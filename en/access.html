ACCESS

The drive through the hills of Biei is part of the journey. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is an 18-minute drive from Asahikawa Airport, 10 minutes from Biei Station, 35 minutes from Asahikawa Station, 40 minutes from the lavender fields of Farm Tomita in the Furano area, and 20 minutes from central Higashikawa. Here is how to get here with peace of mind.

Please note

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is in Nakaubakubetsu, a small settlement in Biei. There is no public transport to the property, so a rental car is essential (taxis are also possible). It is an 18-minute drive from Asahikawa Airport and 10 minutes from Biei Station. Close, yet quiet: enjoy a stillness you can only reach by car. There are few street lights nearby and it gets very dark after sunset, so we recommend arriving before dark.

Base

Biei and Nakaubakubetsu as your base

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a 10-minute drive from central Biei and sits roughly midway between the main sights. With a rental car, you can easily get around Biei, Furano, Asahikawa and Higashikawa.
