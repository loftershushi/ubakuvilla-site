EXPLORE

From UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, a private house in the forests of Biei, you can reach Biei and Furano classics like the Blue Pond and Shikisai-no-Oka, as well as everyday places like Biei Town Library and Kitoushi Forest Kitoron in Higashikawa. Here are ways to enjoy Biei, whether you stay two nights or a month.

Base

Biei and Nakaubakubetsu as your base

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a 10-minute drive from central Biei and sits roughly midway between the main sights. With a rental car, you can easily get around Biei, Furano, Asahikawa and Higashikawa.

Short Stay

The classics of Biei and Furano in 2 to 3 nights

On a first visit to Biei, start with the hills, the pond and the flower fields. Staying at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 means you won't miss the first light of morning or the quiet of dusk.

Hills

Shikisai-no-Oka and its colorful flower fields, the gentle rolling Patchwork Road, and Takushinkan, where you can see the Biei landscapes photographed by Shinzo Maeda: driving the hills is the great pleasure of this area. Early morning is quiet, and the light is at its most beautiful.

Blue Pond

The Blue Pond needs no introduction. It shows a completely different face in each season, from the contrast of green and blue in summer to illuminated snow scenes in winter. Add Shirahige Falls and Shirogane Onsen for a half-day trip.

Furano

Farm Tomita is a 40-minute drive away, and Furano Station a 50-minute drive. In summer, the lavender fields of Farm Tomita in Nakafurano spread as far as you can see, filling the air with a sweet scent. Add Ningle Terrace, with its craft cabins in the forest, and rafting on the Sorachi River for an active day.

Onsen

After a full day, head for a hot bath. Unwind at Kitoushi Forest Kitoron, a day-use bathing facility supervised by architect Kengo Kuma, have dinner there, and then return to UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘.

Town

Biei Station, with its striking stone building, is a 10-minute drive from UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘. Beyond the hills, walking the small town around the station is one of the pleasures of Biei.

Winter

In winter, you can watch penguins walk through the snow at Asahiyama Zoo. Asahikawa Station is a 35-minute drive from UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, making it a good outing on a snowy day.

Long Stay

A week or more, living here

The real charm of Biei begins after you have seen the sights. Leaving days unplanned is the greatest luxury of a long stay.

Slow Days

An afternoon with your own book at the small but cozy Biei Town Library. A coffee at KONON Cafe, hidden in the forest, simply watching the trees. A day of pure, unplanned luxury.

Higashikawa

Visit Sento Pure II in Higashikawa, the "Town of Photography," and the Asahikawa Design Center, with its furniture and crafts in Hokkaido wood. A day in these towns of makers adds depth to your stay.

Daily Life

Buy local ingredients at the supermarket in central Biei and cook in the full kitchen. With a washing machine, you can pack light. Everything is here to live, not just travel.

Workation

With high-speed Wi-Fi and a dedicated workspace, you can work on weekdays and head for the hills on weekends. The 100-inch projector works for movie nights and meetings alike.

Four Seasons

Biei and Furano through the seasons

The same place becomes an entirely different landscape as the seasons change. In Biei, there is something new to discover on every visit.

Spring｜Apr–May

As the snow melts, the land comes back to life. Seidai Dam Park, on the way to UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, is the place to see cherry blossoms in Biei.

Summer｜Jun–Aug

The flower fields bloom all at once and the green is at its deepest. The lavender fields of Farm Tomita are a 40-minute drive away.

Autumn｜Sep–Oct

Harvest season turns the hills gold. The air is clear, and you can see all the way to the distant mountains.

Winter｜Nov–Mar

A world of white. At night, you are wrapped in starlight and deep silence.

FAQ

Questions about visiting Biei

Do I need a rental car in Biei?
Yes. There is no public transport to the property, and the sights of Biei are hard to reach without a car, so a rental car is essential. Rentals are easiest at Asahikawa Airport or Asahikawa Station.
How far are the Blue Pond and Shikisai-no-Oka?
From UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, the Blue Pond is a 28-minute drive and Shikisai-no-Oka a 25-minute drive. Half a day is enough to enjoy both at an easy pace.
Where can I go on a rainy day?
There are plenty of indoor options, including Takushinkan, Biei Town Library, Sento Pure II in Higashikawa and the Asahikawa Design Center. A day with the 100-inch projector at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is also a good choice.
Can I go sightseeing in winter?
Of course. You can enjoy winter scenes like the illuminated Blue Pond and endless snowy landscapes. The sun sets early in winter and there are few street lights nearby, so we recommend arriving before dark.
