Choosing a Private Villa or Vacation Rental in Biei4 Things to Check, and a Hokkaido Stay for Up to 10 Guests

When you choose a private villa or vacation rental in Biei, these four things make the difference.

  • Bedrooms and beds: Is there a bed or futon for everyone? How many separate bedrooms are there?

  • Car and parking: You need a car to explore Biei’s hills and the Blue Pond. How many cars can you park, and how far is the airport?

  • Kitchen: Can you cook for a large group, and is there a table where everyone can sit together?

  • Longer stays: What is the minimum stay? Is there a washing machine and other things you need for a longer trip?

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a private villa in Biei that one group of up to 10 guests rents as a whole house, one group per day. It has three bedrooms and a flexible space, a full kitchen and free parking for two cars, with a minimum stay of two nights. It is about an 18-minute drive from Asahikawa Airport. Below, we go through the four points using the villa as an example.

【How is a private villa in Biei different from a hotel?】

A private villa (whole-house rental) means one group uses the entire building. You never share hallways or baths with other guests, so even a large group can relax as if at home.

  • The whole house is yours: UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 takes one group per day. The living room, kitchen, bath and garden are for your group only.

  • You cook for yourselves: Meals are not included. You are free to use the kitchen, cookware and tableware (seasonings are not provided).

  • Self check-in: There is no key handover. You open the smart lock with a code sent the day before arrival.

【How many people can stay? Check the bedrooms and beds】

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 has bedding for 10 guests, in three bedrooms and a flexible space. For a large group, it is worth checking not only the maximum number of guests, but also the number of bedrooms and beds.

【How can 10 people share the rooms?】

There are two bedrooms on the first floor, and one bedroom and the flexible space on the second floor. Because the floors are separate, two families or a group of different ages can each have their own space.

  • Two families: one family in Bedrooms A and B on the first floor (up to 6), the other in Bedroom C and the flexible space on the second floor (up to 4).

  • A group of 10 friends: 4 in Bedroom A, 2 each in Bedrooms B and C, and 2 in the flexible space.

During the day, the flexible space also works as a workspace, with a desk and chairs for two.

【How many cars can you park? How far is the airport?】

Biei’s sights, its hills and ponds, are spread over a wide area. There are no trains or buses near the villa, so please come by rental car.

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 has free parking for two cars on site, for guests only. If you are a large group, please come in no more than two cars.

【What can you cook in the kitchen?】

With a large group, it is easier to cook and eat at the villa on some days rather than eating out for every meal. The kitchen at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 has a gas stove, a microwave oven, a rice cooker and an electric kettle, along with cookware, dishes and cutlery.

The dining area has a large table where everyone can eat together. The supermarket in central Biei is about a 10-minute drive away, so if you shop before check-in, you can start cooking as soon as you arrive.

【What is the minimum stay? What about longer stays?】

The minimum stay at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is two nights. With a washing machine, a bathroom dryer and high-speed Wi-Fi, the villa also suits stays of a week or longer. Discounts are available depending on the length of your stay. Please tell us your dates through the contact form.

For longer itineraries, see One week in Biei and One month in Biei.

【FAQ】

Q. Is there a private villa in Biei for 10 people?
A. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 can be rented as a whole house by one group of up to 10 guests per day. There is bedding for 10 in three bedrooms and a flexible space.

Q. Can we stay with children?
A. Yes. Up to two children aged 5 and under stay free when sharing a bed; children aged 6 and over pay the adult rate. A baby cot and a high chair are available (one each).

Q. Are meals included?
A. No. You are free to use the kitchen, cookware and tableware. Seasonings are not provided, so please bring your own.

Q. Is there parking?
A. Yes, free parking for two cars on site, for guests only.

Q. Can we bring pets?
A. So that guests with animal allergies can also stay comfortably, we do not allow pets.

Q. What is the minimum stay?
A. Two nights. Discounts are available for longer stays.
