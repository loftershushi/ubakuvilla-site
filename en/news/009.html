One week in Biei: a mid-length stay from a private villa in the forest

"I wish I could stay in Biei a little longer."

Have you ever felt that way? Instead of looking at the hills, taking a photo and moving on to the next stop, this is a week of letting yourself go with the rhythm of Biei. Fields in the morning mist, the changing colors of the sky, a chat with the locals. Here is the Biei you can only meet by staying.

This 7-day model plan stays at a private villa in Biei and takes you as far as Furano and Asahikawa.

DAY 1 │ Arrive and settle into the villa
About 18 minutes by rental car from Asahikawa Airport, or about 35 minutes from Asahikawa Station. Stock up on a week's worth of food at Fuji Supermarket, then check in. Once you have unpacked at the villa in the forest of Biei, there is no need to hurry tonight. Watch a film on the 100-inch projector and let the Biei night settle in slowly.

DAY 2 │ Around the hills of Biei
Shikisai-no-Oka, the Patchwork Road, the Blue Pond. The privilege of a long stay is visiting the classic spots without watching the clock. Go out early in the morning to avoid the crowds, and you can have the ridgelines of the hills rising in the light all to yourself. In the afternoon, follow any side road that catches your eye and find a view of your own.

DAY 3 │ Out to Furano
Summer in Furano is full of color and scent. At Farm Tomita the lavender fields wrap you in their sweet fragrance, and in the forest of Ningle Terrace you step into the world of a popular TV drama ("Mori no Tokei"). In the afternoon, raft down the Sorachi River with Raft&Crew and give two hours to something completely different. Don't forget a change of clothes.

DAY 4 │ A day of art and hot baths
In the morning, visit Takushinkan Photo Gallery, a renovated former school, and spend a quiet moment with the Biei landscapes Shinzo Maeda photographed over many years. In the afternoon, head to Sento Pure II, a cultural complex in the neighboring town of Higashikawa, and relax in a space that brings together a library and galleries. In the evening, go to Kitoushi Forest Kitoron, a hot bath facility supervised by Kengo Kuma, unwind and have dinner there too.

DAY 5 │ A slow day in Biei
Make a day on purpose with nowhere to rush to. In the morning, go to Biei Town Library. It is small but comfortable, so read a book you brought or pick up one about the area. In the afternoon, return to the villa, sip coffee on the engawa veranda and simply look at the hills. A luxurious, unplanned day that only a longer stay allows.

DAY 6 │ Culture and food in Asahikawa
Drive about 30 minutes from Biei to Asahikawa. Take your time looking at furniture and crafts made from Hokkaido wood at the Asahikawa Design Center, then enjoy the behavioral exhibits at Asahiyama Zoo. In the late afternoon, tour Otokoyama Sake Brewery and taste its sake. Back at the villa in Biei, raise a quiet toast with the local sake you bought in Asahikawa.

DAY 7 │ The last morning
Make the most of the time before check-out and go back to the hills once more. The view of Biei in the morning light will surely stay with you. A whole week will have given your trip a depth you can only find here.

What a one-week stay makes possible

  • A plan that doesn't depend on the weather: relax indoors when it rains and head for the views when it's clear

  • Cooking with local supermarket ingredients in a private villa with a full kitchen

  • Visiting the classic spots outside busy hours, when no one else is there

  • Seeing every face of Biei: morning, afternoon, evening and night

FAQ

Q. Is there a private rental house in Biei?
A. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a fully private villa in the forest of Biei, rented as a whole house. It takes 1 to 10 guests.

Q. Is there accommodation in Biei for long stays?
A. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 welcomes stays of a week or longer. The kitchen and cookware let you cook for yourself, and high-speed Wi-Fi makes remote work easy.

Q. What is a good private rental in Biei?
A. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, in the Ubakubetsu area of Biei, is a private rental villa renovated from a farmhouse built in 1968, passed down for more than half a century. You can enjoy your stay in Biei in a completely private space.

Q. How far are Furano and Asahikawa from Biei?
A. From Biei, Furano is about 40 minutes by car and Asahikawa about 35 minutes. With a rental car you can travel freely around northern Hokkaido.
