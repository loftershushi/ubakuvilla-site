Stargazing in Biei, HokkaidoWhen to See the Milky Way, and Starry Nights at a Private Villa

Looking for a place to see the stars in Hokkaido? Here are five things to know.

  • The Milky Way is easiest to see on clear, moonless nights in summer (around June to August).

  • In winter the air is crisp and clear, and bright stars stand out sharply. It is very cold outside, so enjoy them a little at a time.

  • Plan your dates around the new moon for the best view.

  • The countryside of Biei, away from city lights, is very dark and quiet at night. The outdoor light at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 turns off automatically at around 11 p.m.

  • UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a private villa for one group per day. You can see the stars from the windows, and 1 to 10 guests have the garden and the night sky to themselves.

[What our guests say]

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a private villa in Nakaubakubetsu, a small farming community in Biei. We renovated a farmhouse built in 1968, passed down for more than half a century, and opened on January 26, 2026. Around us there is only forest and farmland, and almost no streetlights.

A guest who stayed several nights in August 2026 wrote in their Airbnb review that the stargazing at night was first-class. The photos of the night sky above were also taken by a guest during their stay.

For guests who come for the stars, this article explains when and in what conditions the stars are easiest to see, and how to spend a starry night at the villa.

[5 things to check when choosing a stargazing stay in Hokkaido]

Hokkaido has many places with beautiful night skies. When you choose where to stay, check these five points to see whether you can really enjoy the stars.

  1. Is it away from city lights? The sky is darker in the countryside or the forest than in the center of a town or hot spring resort. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is in the forest, about 10 minutes by car from central Biei.

  2. Do the outdoor lights go off at night? Parking or building lights left on all night make stars harder to see. The outdoor light at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 turns off automatically at around 11 p.m.

  3. Are there lights or voices from other guests? At a private villa for one group per day, you do not have to worry about lights or voices from other rooms.

  4. Can you see the stars without going out? If you can look up from the garden or the windows, there is no need to drive at night, which is reassuring in the cold season. At UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 you can see the stars from the windows.

  5. Is there a way to learn about the stars nearby? Biei has a stargazing night tour for guests staying in the town.

[When and how to see the stars]

Summer for the Milky Way
The brightest part of the Milky Way rises high in the night sky in summer (around June to August). Summer nights in Biei are cool and comfortable for looking at the stars outside.

Winter for sharp, bright stars
In winter the air is dry and clear, and bright stars such as Orion stand out sharply. It is very cold outside, so go out briefly in warm clothes, or watch from the window.

Choose a clear night near the new moon
Stars are harder to see when the moon is bright. Choose dates near the new moon, and check the weather forecast for a night with few clouds.

[A starry night at the villa]

Evening: arrive while it is light
There are no shops near the villa. Pick up groceries at the supermarket in central Biei (about 10 minutes by car) and arrive before dark. Check-in starts at 15:00.

Night: after dinner, wait for the outdoor light to go off
The outdoor light turns off automatically at around 11 p.m. Switch off the lights in the room too, and give your eyes 10 to 15 minutes to adjust. You will see many more stars.

Stargazing tips

  • Dim your phone screen. A bright screen resets your eyes' adjustment to the dark

  • Use your phone's night mode or a tripod to photograph the stars

  • Summer nights can be cool, so bring a jacket. In winter, wear a hat, gloves and a warm coat

  • On cold nights, watching from the window works well too

Join a stargazing tour
In Biei, there is also a night tour (in Japanese) where a guide shows you the night sky. It is for guests staying in Biei. Hearing stories about the constellations makes the stars even more fun.

[Things to keep in mind]

Drive slowly at night
There are almost no streetlights around the villa. Rather than driving somewhere to see the stars, it is safer to watch from the garden or the windows.

Stay off farmland and private property
Most of Biei's hills and fields are private land. Enjoy the stars from the villa grounds or from the road.

Arrive before sunset in winter
The sun sets early in winter. Plan your departure from the airport so that you arrive while it is still light.

[FAQ]

Q. Can you see the Milky Way from UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘?
A. Yes, on clear nights when the moon is not bright. The photos of the night sky in this article were taken by a guest during their stay.

Q. When is the best season for stars in Hokkaido?
A. Summer (around June to August) for the Milky Way, and winter, when the air is clear, for sharp, bright stars. In any season, clear nights near the new moon are best.

Q. Does the outdoor light turn off?
A. Yes, automatically at around 11 p.m. After that, there is almost no light around, and the night is very dark.

Q. Can I see the stars from inside?
A. Yes. On cold nights, turn off the lights and watch from the window.

Q. How many guests can stay?
A. From 1 to 10 guests. The whole house is rented to one group per day. The minimum stay is 2 nights.

Q. How far is it from Asahikawa Airport?
A. About 18 minutes by car. There is no public transport, so please rent a car.
