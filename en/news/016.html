Winter in Biei at a Private VillaA 3-Day Itinerary for Snow, Skiing and Hot Baths

Staying at a private villa in Biei this winter? Here are five things to know.

  • The best season is December to February, with snowy hills, the illuminated Blue Pond, the penguin walk at Asahiyama Zoo and skiing.

  • The countryside of Biei, 18 minutes by car from Asahikawa Airport, makes an easy base. Ski areas are 30 to 60 minutes away, and a hot bath about 30 minutes.

  • In December the sun sets around 4 p.m. Plan to reach your villa before dark.

  • In a whole-house rental, you can wash and dry wet ski wear, and stay in for dinner on snowy nights.

  • UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 hosts one group per day, 1 to 10 guests, with the whole house to yourselves. It suits families and groups of friends.

[From a villa that opened in winter]

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 began welcoming winter guests with a pre-opening in December 2025 and held its grand opening on January 26, 2026. It is a private villa in the forests of Biei, a renovated former farmhouse built in 1968 and passed down for more than half a century.

Here are some of the winter bookings and questions we have received:

  • In March, with snow still on the ground, a guest booked a one-week stay for the next winter.

  • A group from overseas staying in January asked how far the ski areas are.

  • A family coming in December with children asked, "Can the kids play in the snow in the garden?" We have a children's sled ready.

  • We also get requests for long stays of ten nights or more to ski.

This guide is based on what we learned in our first winter and the questions guests ask most often.

[A 3-day winter itinerary]

Day 1: From Asahikawa Airport to the villa
Pick up a rental car at Asahikawa Airport and shop for groceries at the supermarket in central Biei, 10 minutes from the villa. Arrive before dark and warm up by the stove. In the evening, share a hot pot and watch a movie on the 100-inch screen.

Day 2: Snowy hills, the Blue Pond and a hot bath
Start with the snowfields around the villa. On clear mornings, the snow sparkles. Before noon, drive through the hills of Biei to the Blue Pond (28 minutes) and Shirahige Falls. In the late afternoon, warm up at Kitoushi Forest Kitoron, a day-use hot bath in Higashikawa (30 minutes), have dinner there and head back.

Day 3: The penguin walk at Asahiyama Zoo, then the airport
Check-out is at 10:00. Watch penguins walk through the snow at Asahiyama Zoo (35 minutes), then head to Asahikawa Airport.

If you ski
Make Day 2 a ski day. For families who just want to play in the snow, nearby Canmore Ski Village is the easiest choice. Stay three nights or more and you can try a different ski area each day.

[Winter days at the villa]

On snowy days, staying in is part of the pleasure of winter in Biei. A single stove in the living room heats the whole house.

  • Watch a movie on the 100-inch screen

  • Cook a hot pot with local vegetables in the full kitchen

  • Play Othello, cards or Jenga

  • Take the kids' sled out into the snowy garden

[Winter tips]

Arrive before dark
The sun sets early in winter, and there are almost no street lights around the villa. Check-in is from 15:00, so decide when to leave the airport and arrive while it is still light.

Shop before check-in
There are no shops near the villa. The supermarket in central Biei is about 10 minutes away by car.

Keep the stove on
Please keep the stove on during your stay to stop the water pipes from freezing. It is set to around 20°C and can go up to 25°C. The warmer ground-floor bedrooms are best for sleeping.

Snow shovels by the entrance
The grounds are not cleared of snow during your stay. If snow piles up, please use the snow tools next to the entrance.

[FAQ]

Q. Is Biei worth visiting in winter?
A. Yes. Snowy hills, the illuminated Blue Pond, the penguin walk at Asahiyama Zoo and skiing are all winter-only pleasures. The best season is December to February.

Q. Are there ski areas near Biei?
A. From UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, Canmore Ski Village is about 30 minutes by car, and Kamui Ski Links and Furano Ski Resort about 60 minutes.

Q. Is the house cold in winter?
A. A single stove in the living room heats the whole house. It is set to around 20°C and can go up to 25°C.

Q. Is the road to the villa cleared of snow in winter?
A. Yes. The town clears the road in front of the villa. Snow shovels are also kept by the entrance for the parking area and doorway.

Q. Can a group of 10 stay?
A. Yes. We host 1 to 10 guests, one group per day, with the whole house to yourselves. There are three bedrooms and a flexible room with bedding for 10. The minimum stay is 2 nights.

Q. With 10 guests, will the bathroom be crowded?
A. There is one bathroom with a shower. There are two toilets, one on each floor. With a large group, it helps to agree on a shower order.

Q. Can I stay for a long time in winter?
A. Yes. Discounts depend on the length of your stay. Please send your dates through the contact form.

Q. How far is it from Asahikawa Airport?
A. About 18 minutes by car. There is no public transport, so please rent a car.
