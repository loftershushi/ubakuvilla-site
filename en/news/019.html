What Is Ubakubetsu (宇莫別)?Reading, Location and Origin of a Place Name in Biei, Hokkaido, and the Ubakubetsu River

Ubakubetsu (宇莫別) is a place name in Biei, Kamikawa District, Hokkaido. Here are five things to know.

  • It is read “Ubakubetsu” (うばくべつ). It also appears in addresses, as in Nakaubakubetsu (中宇莫別).

  • It is a quiet rural community in Biei, surrounded by forest and farmland. It is about a 10-minute drive from Biei Station and about an 18-minute drive from Asahikawa Airport (from UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘).

  • The name is said to come from the Ainu words “u-pak-pet.” One interpretation is “rivers that rival each other.”

  • The Ubakubetsu River runs through the community. The Seidai Dam was built across the river, and the area around it is Seidai Dam Park.

  • UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, a private villa, stands in Ubakubetsu. The villa takes its name from this place.

【How to Read It: Ubakubetsu】

宇莫別 is read “Ubakubetsu.” The kanji may look hard to read at first, but like many place names in Hokkaido, they were chosen to match the sounds of an Ainu name.

【Where Is It in Biei?】

Ubakubetsu is about a 10-minute drive from central Biei. It is surrounded by forest and farmland, with almost no shops or streetlights. In return, it is very quiet. At night the stars are beautiful, and when you step outside you can hear the river flowing.

It is also close to Asahikawa Airport, about an 18-minute drive after you land. Shikisai-no-Oka is about a 25-minute drive, and the Blue Pond about 28 minutes.

【Origin of the Name: Ainu “u-pak-pet”】

Many place names in Hokkaido come from the Ainu language. The ending “betsu” (別) comes from “pet,” the Ainu word for river.

Ubakubetsu is said to come from the Ainu “u-pak-pet.” In his book on Hokkaido place names, Hidezo Yamada introduces an interpretation from the history of Asahikawa City: “rivers that rival each other.” It is explained as a river almost as long as the nearby Bebetsu River.

Other researchers have suggested different meanings, and nothing has been settled. There are several theories about the origin of the name.

【The Ubakubetsu River and Seidai Dam Park】

The Ubakubetsu River flows through the community. It also runs near UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, and when you step outside at night, the sound of the river is almost all you hear. We hear it is also popular with anglers in summer.

The Seidai Dam was built across the Ubakubetsu River to send water to the farmland. The area around the dam is Seidai Dam Park, and you always pass by it on the way from central Biei to the villa. In spring, it is the place to see cherry blossoms in Biei. We plan to introduce Seidai Dam Park in more detail in another article.

【Scenery of Ubakubetsu: Forest, Fields, Snow and Stars】

Ubakubetsu shows a side of Biei that differs from the famous sightseeing hills: the landscape of everyday rural life.

  • Forest, fields and pastures: The villa is surrounded by forest and farmland, and there is a pasture with cows nearby. The greens are deep in summer, and the trees turn color in autumn.

  • Snow season: In winter, everything is covered in snow, a silent white landscape.

  • Starry skies: City lights do not reach here, so stars are easy to see on clear nights. The villa’s outdoor lights turn off automatically around 11 p.m. Read more in our article on stargazing.

Most of Biei’s hills are private farmland. When enjoying the view, please stay on the road and do not enter the fields.

Q. Is there anywhere to stay in Ubakubetsu?
