Our guests

More than 80% of our guests come from outside Japan. We have welcomed guests from many countries and regions, including Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia, South Korea, the United States, Australia, France, the Netherlands and Ireland.

We are truly grateful that a house renovated from a farmhouse built in 1968, passed down for more than half a century, received the highest ratings on two world-class booking platforms only six months after opening.

Why guests choose us

What UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 offers is not "a trip packed with sightseeing" but "a stay that gives you back the margins of everyday life".

A living room facing the farmland of Biei, a 100-inch projector, and a kitchen where you can enjoy cooking for yourself. We kept "the weight of the past that a new building cannot have" and carefully layered modern comfort on top. Not the special occasion of a hotel, but an extension of everyday life, like a holiday home. We feel that the sense of "coming home" the moment you open the door reaches our guests, whatever their country or culture.

Central Biei is about 10 minutes away by car, and the lavender fields of the Furano area (Farm Tomita) about 40 minutes. You can reach Biei's famous spots, such as the Blue Pond, the Patchwork Road and Shikisai-no-Oka, as well as the lavender fields of Furano, yet when you come back to the villa, only quiet remains. This is time made possible by a private villa for one group per day.

Reviews from our international guests most often praise the cleanliness, the quiet and "time to live as if you belonged here".

FAQ

Q. How many guests can stay? From 1 to 10 guests. The villa has 4 rooms and is rented to one group per day. It also suits groups and families on long stays.

Q. How do I get there from the airport or station? About 18 minutes by car from Asahikawa Airport, about 10 minutes from Biei Station, about 35 minutes from Asahikawa Station, and about 40 minutes from the lavender fields of the Furano area (Farm Tomita).

Q. Do I need a rental car? Yes. There is no public transportation nearby, so we assume guests arrive by rental car. We recommend arranging one at Asahikawa Airport.

Q. How does check-in work? It is a fully self-service check-in with no staff on site. Follow the instructions we send you in advance and arrive at your own timing. We are available by message throughout your stay.
