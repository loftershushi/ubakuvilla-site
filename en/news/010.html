Getting to Biei and Furano: how to reach UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘

If you are looking for a private rental house in the Biei and Furano area, the first question is probably how to get there. Biei and Furano are in northern Hokkaido, but from Asahikawa Airport they are much closer than you might think. This article explains how to get from Tokyo or Sapporo to UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, route by route.

Via Asahikawa Airport (recommended)

Asahikawa Airport is the most convenient gateway to a private villa in the Biei and Furano area. The flight from Haneda Airport to Asahikawa Airport takes about 1 hour 30 minutes. From Asahikawa Airport, UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is about 18 minutes by rental car, and Furano Station about 50 minutes. You can pick up a rental car right at the airport, which is ideal for group trips.

Main airlines: ANA, JAL, AIRDO (relatively affordable) and Jetstar (a low-cost carrier and the cheapest option)

From Tokyo

Via Haneda Airport
About 35 minutes from Tokyo Station to Haneda Airport. About 1 hour 30 minutes from Haneda Airport to Asahikawa Airport. About 18 minutes from Asahikawa Airport to UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘. Flying from Haneda is the smoothest way to reach UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘.

Via Narita Airport
About 1 hour from Tokyo Station to Narita Airport. About 1 hour 30 minutes from Narita Airport to Asahikawa Airport. About 18 minutes from Asahikawa Airport to UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘.

From Sapporo

Via New Chitose Airport
About 2 hours 30 minutes by rental car from New Chitose Airport. This also works if you want to combine sightseeing in Sapporo with a private stay in Biei and Furano. If there is a direct flight to Asahikawa Airport, however, flying there saves a great deal of time.

From Sapporo Station
About 1 hour 30 minutes from Sapporo Station to Asahikawa Station on the Kamui or Lilac limited express. From Asahikawa Station, UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is about 35 minutes by rental car.

About rental cars

If you are staying at a private rental house in the Biei and Furano area, a rental car is a must. There is no public transportation to the Ubakubetsu area, and getting to nearby spots also means driving. The major rental car companies all have offices around Asahikawa Airport and Asahikawa Station.

Summer (July–August) and winter (December–February) are high season, so book early. In winter especially, choose a 4WD car with studless winter tires.

Finding UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘

Search for "UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘" on Google Maps to get the exact route. We recommend shopping at a supermarket in Biei before check-in. There are no shops within walking distance of the villa, so please bring your food and drinks with you.

For more details, see our Access page.

Seasonal notes

Summer (June–August)
Road conditions are good. In July, the lavender season, there are many visitors and the popular spots get crowded. Traveling early in the morning is best.

Winter (December–February)
Watch out for icy roads and snow, especially at night and early in the morning. A 4WD car with studless winter tires is essential.

FAQ

Q. How can I stay in a private rental house in Biei or Furano?
A. UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a fully private villa in the forest of Biei, rented as a whole house. It is about 18 minutes from Asahikawa Airport and well placed for trips to Furano.

Q. Can I get to Biei and Furano by train or bus?
A. The JR Furano Line takes you to Biei Station and Furano Station, but there is no public transportation to the Ubakubetsu area where UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is located. We strongly recommend a rental car.

Q. How long does it take from New Chitose Airport to Biei and Furano?
A. About 2 hours 30 minutes to Biei and about 1 hour 40 minutes to Furano by rental car. If there is a direct flight to Asahikawa Airport, flying there saves a great deal of time.

Q. What should I watch out for when driving to Biei and Furano in winter?
A. Roads can be icy and snowy, so choose a 4WD car with studless winter tires. Take extra care when driving at night.
