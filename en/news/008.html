Biei and Furano in summer: a 3-day, 2-night model course

This is a 3-day, 2-night model course that packs the best of a northern Hokkaido summer into one trip, from the lavender fields of the Furano area to rafting and the Blue Pond, with Biei as your base. You stay at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, a private rental house renovated from a farmhouse built in 1968, passed down for more than half a century. It takes 1 to 10 guests, so small groups and larger ones alike can enjoy the trip at their own pace. That is the real pleasure of having a whole house to yourselves.

DAY 1 │ Arrival and a quiet night

After landing at Asahikawa Airport, pick up your rental car and stop at Fuji Supermarket in central Biei for food and drinks. From the town center, UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is only about 10 minutes away by car.

After check-in, take your time over dinner in the carefully renovated house. End the evening with a film on the 100-inch projector. Shunji Iwai's classic "Love Letter", set in Otaru, Hokkaido, is a fine choice for a quiet night in Biei. It should be a special night, far from the noise of the city.

DAY 2 │ Furano to the full

09:30 Farm Tomita The place to go in Furano. In summer the lavender fields stretch as far as you can see, and the air is filled with their sweet scent. Arrive early to avoid the crowds.

11:00 Ningle Terrace A magical spot where small woodcraft cabins are scattered through the forest. It is also known as a filming location of a popular TV drama ("Mori no Tokei"), and browsing for a favorite piece is a joy.

12:30 Lunch in Furano Fuel up with a lunch made from Furano vegetables.

14:00 Rafting (Raft&Crew) About two hours of rafting down the Sorachi River. A summer activity where getting wet is part of the fun. Bring a change of clothes.

18:30 Kitoushi Forest Kitoron After giving it your all on the river, head to the hot bath facility Kitoushi Forest Kitoron, supervised by architect Kengo Kuma. Relax your body and have dinner there too.

Back at UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, have one more drink before bed as you talk over the day. Enjoy the afterglow of a full day.

DAY 3 │ Around the hills of Biei

09:00 Check-out Load the car and set off straight for sightseeing in Biei.

09:30 Shikisai-no-Oka One of Biei's finest views, with colorful flower fields covering the hillside. Under a blue summer sky it is breathtaking.

10:45 Takushinkan Photo Gallery A gallery in a renovated former school. Spend a quiet moment with the photographs of Biei that Shinzo Maeda took over many years.

11:30 Blue Pond The face of Biei, needing no introduction. In summer the contrast of green and blue is something special, and you never tire of it.

12:30 Yakiniku lunch at Takumiya (匠味屋) A hearty grilled-meat lunch with ingredients from around Biei and Asahikawa. A fitting meal to close the trip.

14:00–16:00 Hill drive Take a slow drive along the Patchwork Road. Free time to enjoy Biei's hills from the car window, including the Ken and Mary Tree and the Seven Stars Tree.

16:00 Potato Com (ポテトコム) A popular stand for fried Hokkaido potatoes. Try them crisp and freshly fried.

16:30 KONON Cafe A hideaway cafe in the forest. The key is to book the last slot at 16:30. End your trip with quiet time in the woods.

17:20 Leave for Asahikawa Airport Take your time choosing souvenirs at the airport, then fly home on the 19:30 flight.
