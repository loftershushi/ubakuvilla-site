PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

1. Compliance and management

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 complies with the laws, guidelines and other rules on personal information. We keep internal rules and training in place to manage personal information properly.

2. Purposes of use

We use the personal information you give us (such as your name, email address and phone number) for the following purposes:

  • To accept and confirm your reservation and contact you about your stay
  • To reply to your inquiries
  • To keep the guest register required by Japan's Inns and Hotels Act
  • To send you news, stay information, campaigns, coupons and similar information by email from UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 and UBAKU (including UBAKU Letter)

We send news and campaign emails to people who have agreed to receive them and to guests who have booked or stayed with us through our official website. To stop receiving them, use the unsubscribe link in any email or contact us at info@ubakuvilla.com, and we will stop.

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is operated by UBAKU. Emails from UBAKU come from the same operator, so sending them does not mean giving your information to a third party.

3. Collection, use and sharing

We collect personal information only as needed to run UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, and use it only for the purposes above. Except where the law allows, we do not give personal information to third parties without your consent. The only exception is contractors chosen by the standards below to carry out these purposes. When we outsource work, we check that the contractor handles personal information properly, sign agreements on confidentiality, no use beyond the agreed purpose and limits on sharing, and supervise them.

4. Security

We manage your personal information carefully and keep taking measures against unauthorized access, loss, destruction, alteration and leaks. Please contact us with any questions, or if you would like us to disclose, correct or stop using your personal information.

5. Our policy

・Compliance
We follow the laws, guidelines and other rules on personal information.
・Rules and training
We set internal rules and work to follow them.
・Management
We appoint a person responsible for personal information and keep improving how we manage it.
・Security
We take necessary and appropriate measures against leaks, loss, destruction, theft, alteration and unauthorized access, and supervise our contractors.
・Collection
We collect personal information by lawful and fair means and keep it accurate and up to date.
・Use
We make the purposes of use clear and use personal information only as needed for them.
・Sharing
Except where the law allows, we do not give personal information to third parties without your consent.
・Disclosure, correction and stopping use
When asked, we respond by our procedures after confirming your identity, except where the law allows otherwise.

6. External services

We use the following external services for the contact form, spam prevention, site analytics and email delivery. All are businesses outside Japan.
・Web3Forms (form delivery / India)
・hCaptcha (spam prevention / USA)
・Google Analytics (site analytics / USA)
・Brevo (email delivery / France)
You can learn about each country's personal information protection system on the website of Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission.

7. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. It collects information with cookies in a way that does not identify you. You can stop this by turning off cookies in your browser or by using the opt-out add-on.

8. Contact

For questions about how we handle personal information, please use the contact form or email info@ubakuvilla.com.
