UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘: A Private Whole-House Rental Villa in Biei, Hokkaido

Nestled in the forests of Biei, Hokkaido, UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 hosts just one group per day. The entire house is yours, for 1 to 10 guests, with self check-in. Here, you can slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with yourself while enjoying the beauty of each changing season.

An 18-minute drive from Asahikawa Airport, 10 minutes from Biei Station, and 40 minutes from the lavender fields of Farm Tomita in the Furano area. The Blue Pond, Shirogane Onsen, and the Patchwork Hills are all within a 30-minute drive. At night, the sky fills with countless stars, and a deep silence envelops you. Escape the noise of everyday life and immerse yourself in the gentle rhythm of nature for a truly special stay.

STORY

A farmhouse built in 1968,
passed down for more than half a century

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 was built as a farmhouse in Nakaubakubetsu, a small settlement in Biei. We kept the original posts and beams, let light into the once-dark rooms, and finished them with white walls and pale wood floors so the forest and hills outside take center stage. A house that stood empty for years is once again a place where people gather.

SEASONS

The same place becomes an entirely different landscape as the seasons change. In Biei, there is something new to discover on every visit.

  • SpringApr–May

    As the snow melts, the land comes back to life. Seidai Dam Park, on the way to UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘, is the place to see cherry blossoms in Biei.

  • SummerJun–Aug

    The flower fields bloom all at once and the green is at its deepest. The lavender fields of Farm Tomita are a 40-minute drive away.

  • AutumnSep–Oct

    Harvest season turns the hills gold. The air is clear, and you can see all the way to the distant mountains.

  • WinterNov–Mar

    A world of white. At night, you are wrapped in starlight and deep silence.
