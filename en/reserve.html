RESERVE

About reservations

Book direct for the best rate.

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is a private whole-house rental in Biei, Hokkaido, for 1 to 10 guests, one group per day. Check availability and prices with the button below.

Before you book

  • Minimum stay: 2 nights (one-night stays are not available).
  • There is no public transport to the property, so a rental car is essential.
  • Self check-in with a smart lock. There are no staff on site.
  • Meals are not included. The kitchen, cookware and dishes are free to use.
  • All guests must upload a valid ID by the day before check-in (required by Japanese law).
Check availabilityBOOK NOW

Reservations are processed on AirHost, our booking system. Credit card payments are accepted.

Long stays

Discounts are available for stays of a week or longer. Please contact us with your dates and we will send you a quote.

FAQ and Terms

Before booking, please read our FAQ and Terms & Conditions. If anything is unclear, feel free to use the contact form.
