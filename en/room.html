ROOM

Warm wood meets modern design. The living room has a 100-inch screen that feels like a private cinema, and the modern kitchen, bath and washroom are ready for longer stays. Carpeted bedrooms with indirect lighting and a flexible room for work or yoga give 1 to 10 guests plenty of space. Beyond the windows, a garden opens onto the forest, and the seasons feel close.

Story

A farmhouse built in 1968,
passed down for more than half a century

UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 was built as a farmhouse in Nakaubakubetsu, a small settlement in Biei. We kept the original posts and beams, let light into the once-dark rooms, and finished them with white walls and pale wood floors so the forest and hills outside take center stage. A house that stood empty for years is once again a place where people gather.

See the renovation (UBAKU Design, in Japanese)

Living Room

At the heart of the house is a living room with a large 100-inch screen, a private theater for films and sports. Gather with friends, or enjoy a quiet movie with family on a night of your trip. By day, light pours through the large windows and the room becomes a lounge in the soft sun.

Kitchen

We took great care with everything that supports daily life here. The kitchen has a gas stove, an oven and even a rice cooker, so you can cook with vegetables and dairy from the nearby supermarket. A meal around the large dining table can become the best memory of your trip.

Bedrooms

The bedrooms are finished in calm colors and soft light, so each day can end in comfort. Carpet underfoot and gentle indirect lighting give them the feel of a fine hotel. Sleep deeply in the quiet, and wake the next morning to birdsong.

The villa sleeps up to 10 guests, with bedding for 10. Up to 2 children aged 5 and under stay free when sharing a bed. One baby cot and one high chair are available.

Flexible Space

Arrange this room however you like. Use it as a workspace, roll out a yoga mat for a small studio, or sleep here when your group is larger. It is a space that gives your days a little margin.

Books & Games

A small bookshelf in the living room holds photo books about Biei and Hokkaido's nature, books on architecture and design, and novels to read slowly on a trip. Find something to unwind with on a quiet evening or during a daytime break. Othello, playing cards and Jenga are here for you too.

Amenity / Facilities

With high-speed Wi-Fi, a washing machine, a refrigerator and a microwave, UBAKU Villa 宇莫別荘 is ready for long stays. The bathroom has a shower and bathtub, and towels, slippers, body soap and shampoo are provided. A stove in winter and air conditioning in summer keep the house comfortable all year. Enjoy a cinema-like evening on the 100-inch screen in the living room. Check-in is by smart lock, so you can arrive and settle in at your own pace.
